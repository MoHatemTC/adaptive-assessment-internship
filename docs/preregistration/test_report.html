Primary objective

The correlation that decides whether corroboration can work at all

Requiring K independent observations only reduces the error rate if those observations are independent. If a candidate's misjudged node drives every inference drawn from them, more observations buy nothing. The pre-registration sets the stop condition at r > 0.4.

It rose and its interval narrowed at every step as harder personas were added, and it stayed far above the line even on the arm with a genuinely noisy grader. A result that strengthens as the evidence broadens is the opposite of one that depends on where it was found.

Safety rates

A perfect graph still fails the gate by four times

The two data-generating arms decompose the error. DGP-2 has 8 of 30 prerequisite edges wrong and a noiseless grader; DGP-3 has every edge correct and a noisy one. Roughly half the error comes from each source — and neither half alone comes close to passing. That removes the most attractive remaining hypothesis: this is not an edge-quality problem that better authoring would fix.

Operating characteristic · deliverable 4

The confidence gate works, saturates at 0.90, and is still not enough

The confidence threshold is the only factor left with reachable levels — corroboration cannot be satisfied on this graph, depth beyond one hop is below the weight floor, and decay collapses to an on/off switch. Swept on DGP-3, the one arm where confidence has any variance.

P09 defeats the gate until it is maximal. From 0.80 to 0.95 its rate barely moves while P01's has already fallen by two thirds. Only C = 1.00 touches it. A candidate who is confidently graded and wrong is precisely the candidate a confidence gate cannot stop — which is what that persona was designed to expose.

The coverage confound was checked rather than assumed: C also decides direct node status, so a tighter gate could "improve" safety by measuring less. Directly-mastered nodes fall only from 9.24 to 8.90 per session across the whole range, with accuracy flat. The gain at 0.90 is real filtering.

The standing threat to validity
The most tractable finding

Twenty-eight items would make the AI Engineer bank measurable

Sixteen of AIE's 33 sub-competencies cannot reach the precision target with their entire item pool administered — they can only ever stop on the question budget. Thirteen of the sixteen sit in C6, the competency the graph's prerequisite edges mostly connect.

Information adds, so the shortfall is a number and "how many more items" has an exact answer. At the cheapest possible fix — items targeted at the candidate's ability, with the bank's own median discrimination — these are a lower bound:

C6.10 misses the target by 0.005 of a standard error. Nine of its items already exist; a tenth, well-targeted, would make it measurable. This reads like a structural defect and it is a fortnight of authoring.

Known limits of these numbers

  • Every session is simulated. No real candidate has sat this assessment, and grader confidence is generated by the harness. Nothing here transfers to production without the gold set.
  • The upper bounds are optimistic. Clopper–Pearson assumes independent observations; the measured design effect is 9.96, so a correctly clustered interval is roughly 3.2× wider than the ones quoted.
  • The screening activity rule degenerated. The harness is deterministic given (candidate, item) and the centre points differed only in factors that change nothing, so pure error came out as exactly zero and every non-zero effect was marked "active" — including one of 0.0017. Effect sizes are readable; the activity column is not. Fixed for future runs; deliberately not applied retroactively, because re-running one arm and not another would make them incomparable.
  • One published figure was withdrawn. An early 40-session probe put P02's wrong-inference rate at 34% against P01's 16%. At 200 sessions across a complete design it is 16.9% against 15.2%. The probe was noise; P02's real cost is on blocking and accuracy, not inference.
  • Depth and edge stratification cannot be applied retrospectively. The engine discarded traversal provenance before this pass, so no depth- or edge-stratified figure may cite the earlier run as its source.
